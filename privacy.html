What the app does

UBFundManager helps community groups keep records of monthly contributions, expenses, extra income and loans. One person creates the organization and acts as its admin. Other members join with an organization code and can view the records.

Information we store

  • Admin account: email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so we cannot see the password.
  • Records the admin enters: organization name and join code; member names, phone numbers and PINs; contribution, expense, extra income and loan records, including amounts, notes and dates.
  • On your phone: your language choice and the organization you belong to.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone data or advertising ID. It has no ads and no third-party analytics.

If you are an admin, you are entering other people's names and phone numbers. Please make sure the members of your group know and agree.

Fingerprint and screen lock

Admin actions such as saving an expense are confirmed with your fingerprint or phone screen lock. The Android system does this check. The app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives or stores biometric data.

How we use the information

Only to run the features described above. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) on Google's servers and is encrypted in transit. Google processes it on our behalf under its own terms.

Who can see it

  • The admin can see everything, including member phone numbers and PINs.
  • Members who join with the organization code can see names, contribution amounts, balances, expenses, extra income and loans.
  • Phone numbers and PINs are restricted to the admin. Only the admin's signed-in account can add records.

WhatsApp reminders

When an admin taps the WhatsApp button next to a member with unpaid dues, the app opens WhatsApp with a ready message for that member's phone number. The admin sends it. The app sends nothing by itself, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to the message.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until the admin deletes it. An admin can open the app, tap the Account button and delete the admin account together with all data of the organization. This is permanent. If you cannot use the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Security

We limit who can add or change records and rely on Google Firebase's security. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password and phone lock private.

Children

The app is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
