Only admins have an account in UBFundManager, created with an email address. Members join with an organization code and do not have accounts.

Delete in the app

  1. Open UBFundManager on the phone where you are signed in as the admin.
  2. Tap the Account button at the top right.
  3. Enter your password and type DELETE.
  4. Tap Delete and confirm with your fingerprint or screen lock.

What is deleted

  • Your admin login (email address and password).
  • The organization and its join code.
  • All members, including their names, phone numbers and PINs.
  • All contribution, expense, extra income and loan records, and the monthly fee settings.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep no copy. Members of that organization will see that it no longer exists the next time they open the app.

If you cannot use the app

Email trikalbarman@gmail.com from the email address of your admin account, with the subject "Delete my UBFundManager account". Add the organization name if you remember it. We will reply to confirm it is you and delete the account and data within 30 days.

If you are a member

You have no account to delete. Ask your organization's admin to remove your entry, or email us with your name and the organization name and we will help.
